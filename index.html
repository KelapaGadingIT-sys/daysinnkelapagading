A Brilliant Beijing Stay
Haidian District hotel by Zhongguancun and the Summer Palace

Experience the innovative atmosphere of Zhongguancun Science Park at Days Inn by Wyndham Beijing Haidian. Boasting elegant Chinese and European décor, high-floor guest rooms with city views, and free WiFi and parking, our hotel is 32 kilometers from Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) and near Third Ring Road and Suzhouqiao Station. Explore the magnificent Summer Palace, visit Beihai Park, or tour top universities like the Beijing Institute of Technology, Renmin University of China, Peking University, and Beijing Foreign Studies University.

Amenities

Brighten Your Day

Enjoy free WiFi and parking, a breakfast restaurant, and gym

Start your day with a buffet breakfast of Chinese and Western cuisine at our restaurant. Work out in the gym, stay connected with free WiFi and a business center, and freshen your wardrobe with an on-site laundry facility. Each high-floor, non-smoking guest room features city views, a flat-screen HDTV with streaming capability, desk, coffee/tea maker, minibar, bathrobe, slippers, bath products, hair dryer, and bottled water. There’s also polished event space and free parking. If you need anything, our attentive front desk staff is available 24/7.

Local Area

Explore China’s Capital

Visit the Beijing Zoo, the Forbidden City, and Tiananmen Square

While here, say hi to giant pandas at the Beijing Zoo, watch a traditional Beijing opera at the National Centre for the Performing Arts, or shop the night market on Wangfujing Street. At the Summer Palace, enjoy a boat ride on Kunming Lake, view thousands of paintings along the Long Corridor, or visit the teahouses on Suzhou Market Street. See ancient artifacts at the Palace Museum in the Forbidden City, admire Ming Dynasty architecture at the Temple of Heaven, and tour Tiananmen Square, Beijing Olympic Park, and the National Library of China.
